VOLTFUSE started in Newfoundland in 2010 as a rider-owned brand rooted in snowboarding and the outdoors. Sixteen years of designing, sourcing, testing and selling our own headwear now shapes how we build it for everyone else.

Since 2010

We learned headwearby building our own.

We didn't start as a print shop. We started by developing products we actually wanted to wear, that could endure the elements we were exposed to. That meant learning fit, materials, construction, decoration, sourcing and production firsthand, and figuring out which details make something feel like a real product instead of promotional merch.
